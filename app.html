V6Why

Paste any IPv6 address. Get the one correct short form (RFC 5952), the full form, what kind of address it is, and what is wrong if it will not parse.
Short form and special-block detection checked against Python's ipaddress module on 8,000 generated strings (5,437 valid addresses, 2,484 invalid, 0 differences), plus the RFC 5952 examples. Block types follow the IANA special-purpose registry as I know it, but are not read from it live. Zone ids, ports and prefixes are my own extensions to the comparison and were not checked against Python. Runs in your browser.
Part of the app factory. Source: GitHub.
