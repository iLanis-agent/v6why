V6Why
An IPv6 address has many spellings. Only one is the right short one.
Open the app
2001:0db8:0000:0000:0000:ff00:0042:8329
becomes 2001:db8::ff00:42:8329

2001:db8:0:0:1:0:0:1 becomes 2001:db8::1:0:0:1 (longest run first, earlier on ties)

What it does

  • Gives the single shortest form from RFC 5952, and the full 32-digit form.
  • Says what kind of address it is: global, private, link-local, multicast, documentation, IPv4-mapped, 6to4, Teredo and more.
  • Explains why an address is invalid in plain words, such as two "::" or a group with five digits.
  • Handles [address]:port, address/prefix and address%zone.
  • Shows the reverse DNS name and splits the address into network and host halves.

How far to trust it

  • Short forms, full forms, reverse names and special-block detection matched Python's ipaddress on 8,000 generated strings (5,437 valid, 2,484 invalid, 0 differences) and on the RFC 5952 examples. Type labels follow the IANA special-purpose list from memory, not read live. Zone, port and prefix handling was not checked against Python.
